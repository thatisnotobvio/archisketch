# Style DNA

## In short
Fixed across every output. Don't reinterpret or "improve" these per request — if the user wants something different, that's a style-guide change, not a generation choice.

## Medium & Line Quality
- Micron pen / fineliner ink illustration, black ink on white (or faintly textured off-white sketchbook) paper.
- Loose, confident, slightly imperfect hand-drawn outlines — not ruler-straight, not vector-clean. Individual pen strokes should be visible.
- Variable line weight: thicker/bolder lines on foreground edges and deep shadow boundaries, thinner lines on distant or fine detail.

## Shading technique
- Cross-hatching and parallel hatching carry mid-tone value (walls in partial shade, textured materials like rough plaster or stone).
- Hatching density = value: sparse hatching reads lighter, dense crosshatch reads darker.
- Solid black fills are reserved for the deepest shadows only — dark window interiors, recessed doorway shadow. Don't use solid black anywhere else.
- Smooth, flat, directly-lit surfaces (e.g. painted stucco in full sun) stay mostly line-only with minimal hatching, so they contrast against textured materials.

## Color
- The entire image is monochrome black ink.

## Mood
- Light, airy, sketchbook / architectural-diary feel — reads as a quick on-site observational drawing, not a stiff technical rendering or a finished illustration.

## Taboos
- No photorealism, no full-color rendering.
- No smooth digital shading or gradients.
- No visible ruler/vector-perfect lines.
- No watermarks, signatures, or text overlays baked into the image.
