# Recurring Elements: Scale Figure & Foreground Planting

## Description
This skill has no fixed mascot character, but it does have a fixed supporting cast that should appear in nearly every sketch, drawn consistently. Treat these like a lightweight IP: consistent look and role across outputs, not decoration to skip when convenient.

## Scale Figure
- One small, simplified human figure, somewhere in the foreground or midground of the composition.
- Minimal detail — a few economical lines, not a rendered portrait. The figure should read instantly as "a person" at a glance, nothing more.
- Pose: naturalistic and in motion — mid-stride, walking — not posed or facing the camera.
- Role: purely for scale and life in the scene. It should never become the focal point or compete with the building for attention.
- Taboo: don't give the figure a face, don't render clothing detail, don't place more than one or two figures (crowds break the sketchbook feel).

## Foreground Planting
- Include whatever planting is actually present in the source photo — cacti, shrubs, trees, grasses.
- Render with textured, spiky/organic hatch marks that match the plant's real form, not a generic decorative bush shape.
- Sits along the base of the building or at the composition's edges, never blocking the architectural details that matter (windows, doors).

## Ground plane
- Sidewalk, pavement, or path rendered with light hatching texture, a few crack lines or paving joints, and a curb line where the source photo shows one.
- Should feel textured and specific, not a blank line at the base of the building.

## Sky/ Background
- Left completely blank white — no rendering, no clouds, no gradient, no hatching. This is a deliberate contrast against the dense hatching on the building itself, not an omission.
