# Prompt Template

Fill every slot. Keep the assembled prompt under ~150 words — image-gen tools dilute attention on overlong prompts, and every slot here should be one tight clause, not a paragraph.

```text
Micron pen (fineliner) architectural sketch, black ink on white paper. Loose, confident hand-drawn linework, visible pen strokes, cross-hatching for shading — not vector-clean.

Subject:
{building type/massing}, {composition pattern} at {camera angle}, showing {which facades}.

{Surface 1 material}:
{rendering treatment — e.g. mostly line-only, minimal hatching}.

{Surface 2 material}:
{rendering treatment — e.g. dense cross-hatching}. {Distinguishing architectural details — windows, doors, railings, overhangs}.

{Scale figure sentence — pose and location}.
{Foreground planting sentence — plant type and location}.
{Ground plane sentence — texture and any curb/ crack detail}.

Sky and background left completely blank white.

Entire image black-and-white ink except {one named element}, rendered in {one named color} — the only color accent.

Sketchbook, architectural-diary mood.

Avoid: photorealism, full color, digital gradients, ruler-perfect lines, watermarks, extra color accents.
```

## Filled Example
Source: modern two-story cube-form house, white stucco upper volume cantilevered over a textured plaster lower volume, yellow front door, cacti along the base, one pedestrian on the sidewalk.

```text
Micron pen (fineliner) architectural sketch, black ink on white paper. Loose, confident hand-drawn linework, visible pen strokes, cross-hatching for shading — not vector-clean.

Subject: modern two-story cube-form house, three-quarter corner view at eye level, showing front and side facades.

Upper volume: smooth white stucco box cantilevered over the ground floor, mostly line-only, minimal hatching.

Ground floor: rough-textured plaster, dense cross-hatching, narrow windows behind wood louvered shutters. One small simplified human figure walks the sidewalk in the foreground for scale. Spiky cacti and desert shrubs line the house base in textured hatch marks. Paved sidewalk with light hatching and a curb line. Sky and background left completely blank white. Entire image black-and-white ink except the front door, rendered solid yellow — the only color accent. Sketchbook, architectural-diary mood.

Avoid: photorealism, full color, digital gradients, ruler-perfect lines, watermarks, extra color accents.
```
